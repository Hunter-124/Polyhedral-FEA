## The datasets that actually pair geometry, BCs, and structural FEA

1. **SFEM** is the cleanest commercial candidate: STEP, fixed-facet masks, point loads, mesh topology, displacement, von Mises, and full stress tensors under MIT. It is synthetic and its stochastic point-loading distribution is narrower than real fixtures/tractions, but it is immediately ingestible.
2. **SimJEB** is the best *real-design* benchmark: every final STEP is a repaired closed volume, the fixture/load interfaces are consistent, and all four fields are supplied. Its CAD is explicitly non-commercial, and its first-order ~2 mm mesh is not a high-accuracy reference for stress singularities.
3. **DeepJEB** expands SimJEB to 2,138 STEP solids and upgrades to second-order tets/modal results. It is more directly useful than DeepJEB++ because it retains STEP, but it is synthetic, bracket-only, and its ODC-By claim should be reviewed against SimJEB’s GrabCAD NC provenance.
4. **DeepJEB++** is much larger and supplies explicit BC indices and fields, but only as OBJ/NPZ. It can train an auxiliary surface-physics encoder, not validate the actual OpenCASCADE ingestion and volume-meshing path.
5. **FRAMED** has loads and FEA indicators but is a beam-frame parameter table, not a volume-solid mesh corpus.

No public dataset above supplies the label PolyMesh ultimately needs: **accuracy/cost over competing mesh configurations** (`h`, mesher family, element tendency/mix, grading, skin layers, adaptivity, and p elevation). Existing FEA datasets contain one chosen discretization per geometry. They are geometry/BC/reference-field seeds; PolyMesh must still rerun its own campaign sweeps to learn the advisor action.

## Recommended starter corpus for one workstation

### Commercial-development track

| Source | Take now | Candidate parts | Compressed download | Role |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| **SFEM** | All STEP plus raw train/validation FEA archives; skip duplicated preprocessed `.pt` initially | ≈16,000 | **≈6.8 GB** | STEP+BC+field warm start and realistic hotspot targets |
| **MFCAD++** | Entire QUB ZIP | 59,665 | **1.5 GB** | Large, clean machining-feature STEP coverage; synthesize BCs on labeled holes/pockets/faces |
| **ABC** | One 10k STEP chunk only, selected after metadata/license and OCCT filters | 10,000 candidates | **≤1.73 GB** | Human-authored geometry diversity and robustness |

**Total:** about **85,000 candidate STEP files and ≤10 GB compressed**. Expect extraction plus generated meshes/results to consume tens to hundreds of GB, so keep raw archives immutable and materialize only campaign-selected solids. If ABC rights review is not approved, replace it with the MIT MFCAD set or another SFEM slice rather than silently mixing uncertain provenance.

### Non-commercial research benchmark add-on

Add **all 381 SimJEB cases**, downloading clean STEP + FEM + CSV + VTK (about **7.34 GB**). Keep its files, derived labels, checkpoints, and experiment outputs in a separately tagged **NC-only** data lineage. Do not train a checkpoint intended for commercial shipment on SimJEB, Fusion 360 Gallery, CADNET, ShapeNet, or DrivAerNet/++.

## Concrete ingestion and labeling rules for PolyMesh

1. **License gate before geometry gate.** Store dataset, model ID, creator/source metadata, exact license, NC/share-alike flags, and redistribution permission. Training manifests and checkpoints should carry a union-of-input-licenses record.
2. **OCCT solid gate:** accept only one connected `SOLID`/`COMPSOLID` with closed shells, positive volume, finite bounds, valid topology, and no self-intersection; reject sheets, wireframes, mixed assemblies, duplicate coincident solids, and extreme unit/scale outliers.
3. **Deduplicate before splits.** ABC contains many exact/near duplicates and primitives; MFCAD variants can differ only by a feature parameter; Fusion assemblies repeat standard hardware. Hash canonical B-rep topology plus normalized geometric descriptors, then keep duplicate families in one split.
4. **Do not learn from published meshes as “optimal.”** Treat SFEM/SimJEB/DeepJEB fields as reference/physics priors. For every retained STEP+BC case, run PolyMesh’s own mesh-action campaign and label error against a converged high-order/high-resolution solve; include wall time, DOF, failures, and estimator quality.
5. **Map BCs to B-rep faces, not only nodes.** Preserve SFEM fixed-facet masks and SimJEB/DeepJEB hole interfaces by geometric face selection so each candidate mesh receives equivalent loads. For MFCAD++, use feature labels to generate controlled, physically plausible fixture/load pairs on separated face sets.
6. **Keep NC data as evaluation-only unless the product is strictly research.** A model checkpoint trained on NC geometry is itself the risky artifact; merely deleting the raw files later does not cure provenance.

## What this means for PolyMesh

Start with **SFEM + MFCAD++ + one ABC chunk**, not million-scale decorative meshes. This gives a workstation-sized, STEP-native corpus with one genuinely useful BC/FEA source and two complementary geometry distributions. Use **SimJEB only in an isolated non-commercial benchmark lane**. The novel work remains PolyMesh’s own: generating multi-mesher, h/p/adaptivity sweeps and reference-error/cost labels, then learning both a global configuration and scalar isotropic sizing field. None of the public corpora has already solved that mesh-policy problem.
